Redline Radar turns common commercial contract blind spots into a guided, interactive pre-negotiation checklist.

## Key Capabilities

- Structured playbooks for MSA, DPA, and AI addendum reviews.
- Instant risk severity scoring and plain-English fallback language prompts.
- Exportable negotiation notes in clean Markdown for legal counsel or founders.
