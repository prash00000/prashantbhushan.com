Built to stop hallucinated citations before they ever touch a draft memo. CiteFlow parses Markdown or plain-text citations and verifies them against user-supplied primary source bundles.

## Core Design

- Strict source-grounded verification pipeline with confidence badges.
- Standardized citation formatting for briefs, articles, and long-form essays.
- Designed as a modular library and CLI for Markdown-first writers.
