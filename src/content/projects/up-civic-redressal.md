A static-first, mobile-first civic grievance and legal-petition platform built for Uttar Pradesh residents (`360px` viewport friendly).

## Key Capabilities

- **Local Officer Finder:** Resolves the administrative escalation chain (Secretary, BDO, SDM, CDO, DM / EO, Chairman / Municipal Commissioner, Mayor) from local election boundaries.
- **Phone Verification Protocol:** Displays explicit verified date, unverified, or stale (`>90 days`) badges on CUG phone numbers.
- **Zero-Storage Privacy:** Generates formal complaint letters and RTI Form-A applications 100% client-side with zero personal data stored.
- **Bilingual Parity & Statutory Citations:** Full English and Hindi parity grounded in the **UP Janhit Guarantee Act 2011**, **UP Panchayati Raj Act 1947**, and **UP Municipalities Act 1916**.
