Most legal content online suffers from the same flaw: it is written to impress other lawyers rather than help the person building a product or signing a deal.

## Show the Artifact, Not Just the Opinion

Whether I am recording a short YouTube walkthrough of a vibe-coded tool or writing a thread on AI terms of service, the format that resonates most has three ingredients:

- **One concrete problem** pulled from real builder friction.
- **Show-your-work proof**—a code snippet, clause comparison, or live demo.
- **A crisp takeaway** someone can apply in five minutes.

Writing and recording in public forces sharper thinking in both law and software.
