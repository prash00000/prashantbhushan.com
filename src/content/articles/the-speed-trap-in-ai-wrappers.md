When generating features takes minutes instead of weeks, speed stops being a moat. Anyone can spin up an interface before dinner.

What remains scarce is **judgment**: knowing what *not* to build, designing interfaces that feel calm and intentional, and taking responsibility for privacy, reliability, and clear communication when users trust your software.

The best vibe coders don't ship more noise—they use extra speed to buy more time for polish.
