"A fair trial is not the accused's private privilege. Article 21 belongs to the victim too and courts can't ignore the hardship a long, distant trial inflicts. The test isn't just whether justice was done, but whether the person who sought it survived the seeking." — posted on [@Embargoliving](https://x.com/Embargoliving/status/2125611182753280210).
